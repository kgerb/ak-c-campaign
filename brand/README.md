# SPP 2433 logo

`spp2433-icon.png` (header) and `spp2433-logo.png` (footer, with the English tagline "Metrology on flying platforms") are the official SPP 2433 logo in blue with white fill. They were made from `SPP2433_Logo_Blau_Weiss.png` and `SPP2433_Logo_Blau_Weiss_Schriftzug_ENG.png` (tagline below the icon) in the programme's logo package (Nextcloud share of Uni Bremen, folder `Vorlagen_Logos/SPP2433 Logo/Transparent`, fetched 27 Oct 2026; local copy in `/mnt/data/kg281/ak-c/assets/spp2433-official/`): cropped to the logo and scaled to 120 px (icon) and 240 px (logo) high.

The package's usage guide (`00_SPP2433_Verwendungsleitfaden.pdf`) recommends the transparent PNG or the SVG for websites and has no further rules. The brand colour is #0065B1 (`SPP2433_Farbe.pdf`). Since 28 Oct 2026 (Kilian) it appears only inside the logo; the site accents are green.
